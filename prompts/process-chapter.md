# Process Chapter

Using the official NCERT chapter as the canonical source:

1. Confirm the class, subject, chapter number.
2. Extract the chapter in its original instructional sequence.
3. Capture every heading, core concept, definition, diagram, table, activity, example and question.
4. Produce student-friendly explanations without changing the meaning.
5. Identify prerequisite knowledge and common misconceptions.
6. Add only limited authoritative enrichment and label it clearly.
7. Produce a compact assessment blueprint.
8. Produce a Core Concept Checklist containing every concept that must survive into chapter artifacts.
9. Produce a Required Visuals section listing every essential diagram, graph, table or activity visual, including its purpose, required labels and accuracy constraints. Assign stable IDs only to these visuals.
10. Ensure the assessment blueprint covers the chapter's domains, weights, misconceptions and source-appropriate item types.

Return one structured Markdown chapter pack.
