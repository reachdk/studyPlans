📚 Golden Grammar Rules & Syntax Matrix

1. Sequence of Tenses & Stative Verbs
Continuous & Perfect Aspects

• Present/Past/Future Continuous forms describe ongoing actions at a point of time.
• Stative Verb Trap: Verbs of feeling, perception, and possession (know, understand, believe, love, own) cannot be continuous.
• Incorrect: 'She was knowing the alphabet.'
• Correct: 'She knew the alphabet.'

2. Reported Speech Backshift Rules
Direct to Indirect Speech

• Simple Present → Simple Past (is → was)
• Present Perfect → Past Perfect (has worked → had worked)
• Simple Past → Past Perfect (saw → had seen)
• Modal shifts: will → would, can → could, may → might
• In polar questions, introduce if / whether; remove question word order!

3. Conditional Clauses (Type 1)
Real & Possible Future Conditions

• Structure: If + Simple Present, will/can/may + Base Verb (V1)
• Trap: Never use 'will' in the if-clause!
• Incorrect: 'If it will rain, the crops will revive.'
• Correct: 'If it rains, the crops will revive.'
• Unless = If not ('Unless we dig trenches...').

4. Clauses: Noun & Relative Clauses
Complex Sentence Synthesis

• Relative Clause: Acts as adjective. Use who for people, which for things, that for defining essential attributes.
• Noun Clause: Functions as subject, object, or complement of a verb. (e.g. 'What she accomplished amazed everyone.')

5. Subject–Verb Concord Traps
Plural vs Singular Agreement

• Indefinite pronouns like each, every, neither, either take singular verbs ('Each of the boys was present').
• Intervening prepositional phrases do not change subject number: 'The quality of the earthen pots was exceptional' (not 'were').

6. Determiners & Quantifiers
Subtle Distinctions

• Few / Little: Negative connotation (almost none).
• A few / A little: Positive connotation (some).
• Much: Uncountable nouns ('much water').
• Many: Countable plural nouns ('many artisans').

1. Periodic Pen-and-Paper Tests
Weightage: 5 Marks

2. Multiple Assessment
Weightage: 5 Marks

3. Student Portfolio
Weightage: 5 Marks

Purposeful collection of student work over the term: writing samples, worksheets, self-reflections, teacher feedback logs, and creative assignments.

Weightage: 5 Marks

Assessment of Listening and Speaking Skills (ALS), Art-Integrated projects, IKS (Indian Knowledge Systems) research, or technology-assisted script writing.
