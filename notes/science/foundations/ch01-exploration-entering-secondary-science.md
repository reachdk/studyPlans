## 1. Chapter Snapshot & Learning Objectives
* **Nature of Science & Scientific Method**: Systematic inquiry, making observations, asking questions, formulating hypotheses, controlled experimentation, data analysis, and drawing evidence-based conclusions.
* **Quantification and Measurements**: Importance of standard objective measurements over subjective senses; the **International System of Units (SI Units)**.
* **Fundamental Physical Quantities and Base SI Units**: Length (Metre), Mass (Kilogram), Time (Second), Temperature (Kelvin), Electric Current (Ampere), Amount of Substance (Mole), Luminous Intensity (Candela).
* **Laboratory Safety & Experimental Ethics**: Safe handling of chemicals, burners, glassware, precise record-keeping, and reproducible observations.
* **Interdisciplinary Nature of Science**: How Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Earth Sciences interconnect to explain natural phenomena.

```
                      The Scientific Method
                                │
                        [Observation]
                                │
                        [Ask a Question]
                                │
                     [Formulate Hypothesis]
                   (Testable educated guess)
                                │
                    [Design Experimentation]
               (Independent vs Dependent variables)
                                │
                      [Analyze Results/Data]
                                │
                  ┌─────────────┴─────────────┐
        [Hypothesis Supported]      [Hypothesis Refuted]
                  │                           │
          [Formulate Theory]         [Refine Hypothesis]
```

## 3. The 7 Base SI Units of Measurement

| Physical Quantity | Symbol | Base SI Unit | Unit Symbol | Practical Lab Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Length** | $l, s$ | Metre | $\text{m}$ | Vernier callipers & screw gauge for precision. |
| **Mass** | $m$ | Kilogram | $\text{kg}$ | Note: Weight is a force measured in Newtons ($\text{N}$). |
| **Time** | $t$ | Second | $\text{s}$ | Stopwatch with $\pm 0.01\text{ s}$ precision. |
| **Thermodynamic Temperature** | $T$ | Kelvin | $\text{K}$ | $T(\text{K}) = t(^\circ\text{C}) + 273.15$ (No degree symbol used for Kelvin). |
| **Electric Current** | $I$ | Ampere | $\text{A}$ | Measured with an ammeter in series. |
| **Amount of Substance** | $n$ | Mole | $\text{mol}$ | $1\text{ mol} = 6.022 \times 10^{23}$ particles. |
| **Luminous Intensity** | $I_v$ | Candela | $\text{cd}$ | Light intensity in a given direction. |

## 4. Temperature Conversion Rule
* To convert from Celsius ($^\circ\text{C}$) to Kelvin ($\text{K}$):
  $$\mathbf{T(\text{K}) = t(^\circ\text{C}) + 273}$$
* To convert from Kelvin ($\text{K}$) to Celsius ($^\circ\text{C}$):
  $$\mathbf{t(^\circ\text{C}) = T(\text{K}) - 273}$$
* *Example 1*: Boiling point of water $= 100^\circ\text{C} = 100 + 273 = \mathbf{373\text{ K}}$.
* *Example 2*: Freezing point of water $= 0^\circ\text{C} = 0 + 273 = \mathbf{273\text{ K}}$.
* *Example 3*: Room temperature $25^\circ\text{C} = 25 + 273 = \mathbf{298\text{ K}}$.

## 5. Active Recall Checkpoint

1. What are the three primary steps of the scientific inquiry method? *(Answer: Observation $\rightarrow$ Hypothesis formulation $\rightarrow$ Controlled experimentation and verification)*
2. What is the SI unit of thermodynamic temperature? *(Answer: Kelvin / K)*
3. Convert $37^\circ\text{C}$ (normal human body temperature) into the Kelvin scale. *(Answer: $37 + 273 = 310\text{ K}$)*
4. Why is weight in Physics not measured in kilograms? *(Answer: Mass is the quantity of matter measured in kilograms; weight is the gravitational force acting on that mass and is measured in Newtons)*
5. Name two instruments used to measure length with greater precision than a standard millimetre scale. *(Answer: Vernier callipers and Screw gauge)*
